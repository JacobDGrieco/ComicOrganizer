# Inclusion Rules

Research cutoff: 2026-07-31

This database includes original English-language comic publications that a reader would reasonably want when following Spider-Man, Spider-family characters, major Spider-Man villains, major symbiote characters, and Spider-Man-centered continuity.

## Include

- Ongoing series, limited series, miniseries, maxiseries, annuals, specials, one-shots, Giant-Size issues, original graphic novels, event miniseries, digital-first originals, Infinity Comics, FCBD issues with meaningful original story content, and substantial anthology stories.
- Main Spider-Man and Spider-family publications, including Peter Parker, Miles Morales, Gwen Stacy / Ghost-Spider, Miguel O'Hara, Ben Reilly, Kaine, Cindy Moon / Silk, Spider-Woman characters, Mayday Parker, Spider-Boy, Spider-Punk, Spider-Man Noir, Spider-Ham, and comparable researched cases.
- Spider-Man supporting-character publications when the title or premise is Spider-Man-facing enough that a Spider-Man reader would reasonably expect it, including Black Cat, Mary Jane Watson / Jackpot, and comparable researched cases.
- Major Spider-Man-centered events and crossovers, with core chapters distinguished from required, recommended, and optional tie-ins.
- Symbiote publications only when important to Spider-Man mythology, Spider-family continuity, or a major Spider-Man-adjacent event.
- Event tie-in volumes are included when the event is anchored by a Spider-Man-derived character or symbiote lineage, even if individual tie-ins feature other Marvel heroes.
- Alternate-continuity and all-ages publications when they contain original stories rather than reprints or simple adaptations.

## Exclude

- Trade paperbacks, hardcovers, omnibuses, Epic Collections, Marvel Masterworks, complete collections, compendiums, digests, facsimiles, True Believers reprints, later printings, variant-cover editions, sketch editions, foil variants, and reprint-only Marvel Tales issues.
- Director's Cuts, remasters, recolored editions, treasury editions, or magazine editions whose purpose is primarily reprinting existing stories.
- Later physical printings of digital-first stories already represented by their original format.
- Foreign translations of English-language comics already recorded.
- Every incidental guest appearance in unrelated titles.

## Ambiguous Cases

If a publication mixes reprints with substantial original Spider-Man material, include it once and explain the original content in `notes`.

If a title is marginally related, create a `review.json` item instead of silently excluding or including it.

If sources disagree about issue count, date, numbering, or reading order, record the disagreement and mark the affected record `Conflicting Sources` or `Manual Review`.
